    Known limitations

    • Labels were mined from radiology reports with NLP and are noisy (~90% accurate).
    • Trained on a single US hospital; performance on Indian hospital data (different scanners, demographics) must be validated before any use.
    • Frontal views only; lateral views and paediatric images are out of distribution.
    • Heatmaps show where the model looked, not proof of pathology.

    Provenance

    LungLens is a research prototype and is not a medical device. It must not be used for clinical decisions.
