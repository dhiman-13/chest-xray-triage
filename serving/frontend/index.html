    Known limitations

    • Labels were mined from radiology reports with NLP and are noisy (about 90% accurate).
    • Trained on data from a single US hospital. Performance on other populations and scanners must be validated before any use.
    • Frontal views only. Lateral views and paediatric images are out of distribution.
    • Operating points favour sensitivity, so most studies are flagged for review.
    • Heatmaps show where the model looked, not proof of pathology.

    Provenance

    Not a medical device. LungLens is a research prototype and must not be used for clinical decisions.
